I'm Gaëtan Bervet. I build and operate production machine-learning systems — most
visibly [narcis.ai](https://narcis.ai), a live diffusion pipeline for face-identity
image generation.

My background is infrastructure engineering, applied to ML. The recurring theme here:
most systems underperform because the execution is loose, not because the math is
wrong — so the writing tends toward reading reference implementations against their
source, measuring, and closing that gap.
